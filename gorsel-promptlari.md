## ORTAK BLOK (foto promptlarının sonuna ekle)
```
Photorealistic editorial food photography, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, soft directional window light from the left, subtle film grain, natural imperfections. Strict color palette: sun-dried tomato red (#A8321F), green bell pepper (#4E7D2F), warm dough beige (#E8D5B0), dark anthracite charcoal (#24272B). Dark matte anthracite stone surfaces, charcoal metal trays, dough-beige linen paper. Pizza is hand-made: leopard-spotted blistered crust, glossy melted cheese. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no faces, no stock-photo look, no plastic sheen, no cartoon, no oversaturation.
```

## 1) Logo (vektör stil, ortak blok EKLEME)
```
Logo design for a new pizza-by-the-slice franchise brand named "CRUST". Horizontal lockup: on the left, the minimal pizza slice emblem (a single pizza slice pointing downward, one bite taken from the tip, scalloped crust edge at the top, three small green bell pepper #4E7D2F dots); on the right, the wordmark with the exact letters C R U S T in a heavy geometric sans-serif, tight tracking, and beneath the wordmark a thin bar with a scalloped, bubbly edge like a pizza crust. Flat vector, sun-dried tomato red #A8321F on dough beige #E8D5B0 background. Centered, generous margin, crisp edges, no gradients, no shadows, no mockup, no extra text. Square 1:1.
```
Varyantlar: aynı prompta "dough beige #E8D5B0 logo on dark anthracite #24272B background" ve "all anthracite #24272B on beige" yazarak 2 tane daha iste.
